## Derived summary
The speaker argues that AI-agent use will increase demand for compute, storage, and databases beyond human-driven usage. He describes AI engineering as a broad skill set relevant across software roles, with four core areas: building and deploying uncertain AI applications, software-engineering fundamentals, effective use of coding agents, and “shaping the build” by influencing product and architecture decisions. He says strong practitioners use monitoring, error analysis, and evaluation loops; understand trade-offs such as cost, latency, speed, reliability, security, and rewrite risk; and adjust planning versus rapid prototyping to the project stage. He also contends that human context remains valuable where agents lack customer, market, and organizational knowledge.
