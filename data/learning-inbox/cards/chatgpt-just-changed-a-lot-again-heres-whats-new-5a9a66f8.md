## Learning guide
**The arc**
This roundup asks which AI updates make personal agents more useful in everyday work. The through-line is moving from isolated chats to agents that share context, communicate across channels, and carry out tasks, while judging their value by practical results rather than announcements alone.

**1. Worth trying: agents that reach you where you work**
- **Connect Dot to texting:** In ChatGPT, click your agent’s Dot icon, choose **Setup texting → Get started**, enter your phone number, and verify the **six-digit code**. Press **Open messages** and send the preloaded “hey dot” greeting to start using the messaging channel.
- **Check shared context and connections:** Ask Dot which plugins or connectors it can access through texting. Use a discussion about a video idea, link, or hook to explore whether the conversation carries over your existing personal context and desktop connections.
- **Specify delivery channels:** Give a routine both a schedule and a destination so updates reach you without opening ChatGPT. Examples include an email summary every morning at **9:00 a.m.**, AI news every day at **2 p.m.**, or a short daily recap by text at **5:30 p.m.**
- **Test cross-channel delivery:** From the computer, ask Dot to send a specific greeting to iMessage and look for it on your phone. This establishes the desktop-to-phone path before you use it for something useful, such as notifications about an urgent email thread.
- **Try a bounded coding request:** Send Dot a voice message requesting a **local Codex session** to make minor design improvements to the **Agent Native landing page** and push the changes. Inspect the completed work before treating messaging-based delegation as an established workflow.
- **Explore Duet’s channels:** Use **Claude Duet** to plan work in separate channels rather than managing different agents. Planning a presentation, including from a phone, is a concrete starting point for exploring its persistent-agent experience.

**2. Less friction: phone actions and usage capacity**
- **Find the Siri beta:** Go to **Settings → search for “update” → Software Update → Beta Updates** and check for availability. The setup described requires **iOS 27 developer beta** plus separately enabling the Siri beta.
- **Start with simple Siri actions:** Hold the Siri button to request an internet search, or ask it to open **Claude**. Use the dedicated Siri app to revisit conversations, and keep initial tasks simple rather than assuming full phone control.
- **Describe what you need:** Try opening an app by description instead of its exact name, as with the love-themed app for building apps with AI that opened **Lovable**. For onscreen assistance, ask Siri to research what is currently displayed and then ask follow-up questions.
- **Review plan capacity:** Compare your plan’s current limits before changing subscriptions for more ChatGPT, Codex, or Dot usage. The changes covered include the **$100 per month** plan moving from **5× to 10× Plus** usage and **Pro 200** retaining **20× Plus** rather than falling to **10×**.
- **Explore Grockbot integrations:** Use its Apple integrations for questions about your location and access to the native calendar. These are concrete integration tasks to explore before broader features such as **Pages** or improved video creation, for which no detailed workflow is supplied.

**3. Better choices: test utility before switching**
- **Judge models through practical work:** Do not choose **Gemini 4 Carbon** solely because its model card places it ahead of Claude Opus and GPT6 Astra in all but one category each. Compare practical usefulness, since the earlier **Gemini 4 Argon** also had favorable comparisons without earning the same practical confidence.
- **Make local coding a selection criterion:** If your workflow depends on local coding sessions, establish that capability before replacing **Claude Projects** with Duet. Keep a clear distinction between a persistent, channel-based agent and one that can actually launch local **Claude Code** work.
- **Read financial material for understanding:** Examine the **S-1**, or ask AI to produce a document explaining it, rather than treating an IPO discussion as an investment recommendation. The highlighted **900% revenue retention** figure means the same customer spending **$9 now** for every **$1 a year earlier**; keep that metric separate from explanations of why growth occurred.

**4. Skills to build: implementation and oversight**
- **Map repetitive work:** Examine a department’s tasks to identify where agents could help, including documentation, summarization, translation, customer support, finance, HR and recruiting, operations, and software engineering. Focus on the actual tasks rather than assuming an entire job can be replaced.
- **Practice agent management:** Build skill in configuring agents, checking that they do the right thing, adding workflows, and improving operational efficiency. This turns familiarity with AI into implementation and oversight work rather than just manual task execution.

**Try this first:** Connect Dot to texting, then ask it from your computer to send a specific greeting to iMessage and confirm that it arrives.
