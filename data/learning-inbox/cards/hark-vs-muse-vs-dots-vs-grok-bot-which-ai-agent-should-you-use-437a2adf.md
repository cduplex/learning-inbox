## Practical applications
- Suggestion: Evaluate assistants by matching the task to the claimed strength: personal logistics and UI simplicity for Hark, high-stakes knowledge work and ecosystem integration for Dots, and specialist multi-agent workflows for Grockbot.
- Suggestion: Before connecting an assistant to email, messaging, files, or business systems, assess its data access, memory controls, encryption claims, and ability to approve or inspect outbound actions.
