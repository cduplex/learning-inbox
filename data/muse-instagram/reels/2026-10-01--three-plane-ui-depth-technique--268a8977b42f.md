## Practical takeaways
- Build depth through overlap: background type, midground subject, foreground actions read as three distinct planes.
- Reserve exactly one color for tappable elements; if everything signals, nothing signals.
- Commit to extreme scale contrast instead of medium defaults: one dominant element, small supporting ones.
- Test the planes on a real small screen; depth that needs a large display to parse does not survive on a phone.
