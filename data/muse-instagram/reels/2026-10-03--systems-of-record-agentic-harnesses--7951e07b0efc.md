## Practical takeaways
- Assume external agents will reach your endpoints: design data access with agent consumption as a first-class scenario.
- Build guiding constraints into your platform (what agents may do, not just what data exists) rather than relying on passive storage as a moat.

- Track how benchmark improvements from tool use generalize: an agent with tools is a different system than the base model.
