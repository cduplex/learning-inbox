## Derived summary
Jess Guzik, a career coach, teaches the ORDER framework for delivering bad news to leadership without ruining credibility: Objective, Result, Diagnosis, Explanation, Resolution. She walks through an example: the Q3 objective was $7 million in revenue, the result was $5.5 million, the diagnosis was a larger-than-expected seasonal slowdown, the explanation was that a late summer heat wave increased the slowdown from 2% to 4%, and the resolution was adjusting next year's strategy in two ways based on that learning. Her conclusion is that this sequence transforms bad news into an opportunity to demonstrate strategic thinking and leadership. The delivery is clear and confident, with on-screen text reinforcing each step. This is communication coaching advice from one practitioner; the example is illustrative rather than a case study, and the framework is presented as a method, not as tested research.

## Practical takeaways
- Lead with the Objective so the gap is measured against a shared target, not discovered mid-conversation.
- Separate the Result (what happened) from the Diagnosis (why you think it happened) and the Explanation (the mechanism).
- Quantify the explanation when you can; "2% to 4%" makes the story checkable instead of hand-wavy.
- Always close with a Resolution: two concrete strategy adjustments turn a miss into a leadership moment.
