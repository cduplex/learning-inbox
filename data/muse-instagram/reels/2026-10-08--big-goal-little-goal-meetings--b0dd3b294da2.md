## Practical takeaways
- Open every meeting by naming the Big Goal: the company-level outcome this work serves.
- Follow immediately with the Little Goal: what today must accomplish toward it.
- Use the pairing to create urgency: people lean in when the small ask is visibly connected to the large outcome.
- Prepare the one-sentence Big Goal before the meeting; it should not be improvised on the spot.
