## Practical takeaways
- Most of your audience still treats AI like Google; design training and tooling for that starting point.
- The "real work" tier is where professional value appears: move people from chat curiosity to task-integrated use.
- Plan around jaggedness: choose tools and models per task, not one default for everything.
- Remember the access gap: what you see in a lab or pro tier is not what most users experience, so calibrate expectations.
