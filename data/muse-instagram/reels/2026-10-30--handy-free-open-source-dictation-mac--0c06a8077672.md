## Practical takeaways
- For writing and coding, a keyboard shortcut dictation tool can reduce friction versus opening a separate transcription app.
- Test dictation on proper names and domain terms from your own work, not just clean sentences: that is where accuracy differences show up.
- If you need meeting recordings or phone dictation, check those features separately: commenters flag them as Handy gaps.
- Prefer a local, open source dictation tool when you want speech kept on device, and confirm the model runs locally before assuming it does.
- Compare ergonomics, not just accuracy: a slightly less accurate tool with a better shortcut and editing flow may win in daily use.
